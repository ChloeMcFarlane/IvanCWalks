Choreography, acting &
directing, production.

Every time the pursuit of product overshadows the attention to authenticity an artist loses its wings. If there was anything else we could be doing with our lives we would be, but alas, we are blessed with purpose and burdened with the knowledge of it. The universally acquired taste of talent, skill, discipline, inclusion, and community is the type of palette our kitchen caters to. Our Concept Concerts prove there are still new stories to be told, new songs to be written, new works to be danced within one of the oldest traits of humanity: live theater. In short, we still have our wings and are still reaching for the sky.
